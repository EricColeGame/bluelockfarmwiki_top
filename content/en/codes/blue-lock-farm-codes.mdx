## What Are Blue Lock Farm Codes and Why Do They Matter?

Blue Lock Farm is a Roblox tycoon-style game built around soccer. You buy lockers, hatch anime-inspired players from them, and those players farm balls that get packed into crates. Selling full crates earns Cash, and Cash buys better lockers plus upgrades for crate value, luck, opening speed, movement, and base size. It's a classic loop: earn, reinvest, earn faster.

Codes are the game's freebie layer on top of that loop. They're short strings you enter in the Shop menu for instant rewards, which means you can skip a chunk of the early grind without spending Robux or hours of idle time.

| Stage of the loop | What you do | Cost | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buy lockers | Place new locker stations | Cash | More player slots |
| Hatch players | Open lockers for new units | Time / locker cost | A bigger roster |
| Farm balls | Let players generate balls | Nothing | Balls to crate |
| Sell crates | Ship full crates | Nothing | Cash |
| Upgrade | Improve value, luck, speed, base size | Cash | Higher income per crate |
| Redeem codes | Enter codes in the Shop menu | Nothing | Potions, Tokens, bonuses |

The catch is that codes are usually one-time-use per account, so timing matters. Redeem them while they're live, and redeem them on the account you actually play.

### Grade Tokens and the Ranking Stand

Grade Tokens are the reward most players underrate. You spend them at the **Ranking stand** in the Plaza to grade players, which raises their grade and their Cash multiplier. You *can* roll grades with Cash instead, but according to community reports, high-tier rolls can cost millions each — an amount that eats an entire farming session. The UR grade carries the largest Cash multiplier in the game, so every token you save is money you don't have to grind back.

The smart play is to bank your Grade Tokens until you have a player worth investing in, then spend them in one run rather than sprinkling single rolls across random units.
