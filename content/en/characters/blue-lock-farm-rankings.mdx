Blue Lock Farm rankings are the single biggest multiplier on your income in this Roblox soccer tycoon. A small roster of properly graded players will out-earn a sprawling base full of unranked commons every single time. If you've been buying lockers, hatching players, and selling crates without ever visiting the Ranking stand, you're leaving millions of Cash sitting on the field.

## How the Blue Lock Farm Rankings System Works

Every player you hatch from a locker rolls with a grade attached, and that grade acts as a multiplier on the Cash your players generate while farming balls. To change it, walk to the Ranking stand in the Plaza and roll for a new grade — either with Grade Tokens you've earned for free, or with raw Cash.

Cash rolls are cheap while your players are weak, but the price scales hard. Community reports put high-tier rolls in the millions of Cash each, which is money that would otherwise fund locker upgrades, crate value, opening speed, and base size. Grade Tokens cost you nothing but time. That asymmetry is the whole strategy: spend Tokens on keepers, spend Cash on infrastructure.

| Roll Method | Cost | Best Used On | Watch Out For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grade Tokens | Free — earned from codes, rewards, and milestones | High-rarity players you plan to keep long-term | Burning them on commons you'll replace in an hour |
| Cash rolls | Scales with player tier; reportedly millions per roll at the top end | Cheap filler players early on | Draining your upgrade budget for one bad roll |
| Jackpot Tokens | Free reward tokens | Any roster, when you want a shot at a bonus reward | Chance-based — treat it as a bonus, not a plan |

The UR grade sits at the top of the ladder and delivers the biggest Cash multiplier in the game. Every other grade is a stepping stone toward it.

## Grade Tiers: What Each Band Does for Your Farm

The exact number of grades and their names can shift with updates, so don't treat any tier list as permanent law. What matters is the shape of the curve: each band up adds meaningfully to your multiplier, and the jump at the very top is the one that changes how fast your crates sell.

| Grade Band | Typical Source | Multiplier Impact | Grade It? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Common and uncommon hatches | Small bump — barely moves crate income | No. Sell it or use it as early filler |
| Mid | Rare and epic hatches | Noticeable gain, solid early-game value | Only if it's in your active farming lineup |
| High | Legendary-tier hatches | Big jump; usually your first real money-maker | Yes — this is where Grade Tokens pay off |
| Top (UR) | The rarest hatches in the game | Largest Cash multiplier available | Absolutely — this is the end goal |

Because grades interact with traits, variants, and locker upgrades, a lower grade on a heavily invested player can outperform a higher grade on a throwaway. Judge your roster by what each player earns per minute, not by the color of the tag above their head.

## Blue Lock Farm Rankings Priorities: What to Grade First

With a limited supply of Grade Tokens, order of operations matters more than raw luck. Use these signals to decide which players deserve a roll and which ones should be sold without a second thought.

| Priority Signal | Why It Matters | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Base rarity | Higher-rarity hatches start with stronger multipliers | Grade rarity before volume |
| Locker investment | Players tied to upgraded lockers scale harder as you invest | Match grading to your best lockers |
| Trait setup | Traits stack on top of grade multipliers | Trait a player first, then grade |
| Variant status | Variants are limited and often outperform standard versions | Never skip a variant keeper |
| Lineup role | Only players actively farming balls generate income | Grade the active lineup, not the bench |

### Traits, Variants, and the Real Tier List

Community tier lists almost always rank players by their ceiling — the best possible grade paired with the best possible trait combination. That's useful for planning, but it misleads new players. A top-tier player with no traits and a low grade will lose to a mid-tier player that's fully traited and graded UR.

- **Trait Tokens** come from codes and events — stockpile them before a big hatching session.
- **Variant Tokens** are rare; spend them only on players you're certain to keep.
- **Luck Potions** improve hatch quality, which means fewer wasted grading attempts.
- **Grade Speed Potions** shorten the grind so you can roll more often.
- **Cash Potions** accelerate the upgrade loop that funds everything else.

In other words, the Blue Lock Farm rankings that matter most are the ones inside your own base. A player's theoretical tier only converts into Cash once it's graded, traited, and actively farming.

## Ranking Mistakes That Cost You Real Progress

Most players don't lose because of bad luck. They lose because they spend Grade Tokens in the wrong order, on the wrong players, at the wrong time.

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | The Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Grading commons | Tokens vanish on players you'll replace by dinner | Grade only long-term keepers |
| Cash-rolling high-tier players | Millions per roll stalls your locker upgrades | Save Cash rolls for cheap filler |
| Grading before traiting | You lose efficient multiplier stacking | Trait first, then grade |
| Ignoring codes | Free Grade Tokens expire unused | Redeem everything weekly |
| Grading bench players | Bench players don't farm balls | Grade your active lineup only |
| Chasing tier lists blindly | The meta shifts with every update | Verify against your own earnings |

One more habit worth building: after every update, re-check your best players' actual earnings before you invest further. Numbers in-game beat opinions on a tier list every time.

## FAQ: Blue Lock Farm Rankings

### What is the best grade in Blue Lock Farm rankings?

UR is the top grade and carries the largest Cash multiplier in the game, which makes it the long-term target for every player you intend to keep. Lower bands still help early on, but they're transitional — don't pour your whole Token supply into them.

### Do you need Robux to climb the Blue Lock Farm rankings?

No. Grade Tokens are handed out through codes, milestones, and event rewards, and they cover the same rolls that Cash does. Patient players can reach high grades without spending anything, though it takes longer than paying for shortcuts.

### Should I use Cash or Grade Tokens to rank up a player?

Use Grade Tokens on players you're keeping, and Cash only on cheap filler early in your run. Once rolls start costing millions, Cash rolling a keeper is a fast way to stall your entire base's growth.

### How often do the rankings change?

Every major update can shift the meta — new players, new traits, and balance tweaks all move the goalposts. Community tier lists update quickly after patches, so check the game's Discord before making a big grading decision.

Get your grading order right, stack traits before grades, and redeem every code the day it drops. Do that, and your farm will out-earn bases twice its size.
