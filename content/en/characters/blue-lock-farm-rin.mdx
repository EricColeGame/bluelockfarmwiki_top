## How the Locker-to-Crate Loop Actually Works

Lockers are the engine of everything. Each locker you own hatches players on a timer, those players walk out onto your base and farm balls, and the balls fill crates. Selling a full crate converts them into Cash, which buys more lockers and more upgrades.

Building a Blue Lock Farm Rin setup means understanding that he does not exist in a vacuum — he exists inside a base that has to feed him. The five levers you pull are crate value, luck, opening speed, movement, and base size. Everything else is a support system for those.

## Grading Blue Lock Farm Rin Without Burning Millions

Grading happens at the Ranking stand in the Plaza, and you have two ways to roll: spend Cash, or spend Grade Tokens. For high-tier units, Cash rolls run into the millions per attempt, so a bad streak can wipe out days of farming. The UR grade delivers the biggest Cash multiplier in the game, which makes it worth chasing — just not with money you need for lockers.

| Grading route | Cost per roll | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash rolls at the Ranking stand | Millions of Cash on high-tier units | Late-game players with maxed crate income | Draining the budget you need for lockers |
| Grade Token rolls | Free tokens from codes and rewards | Almost everyone | Limited supply — pick one target |
| Waiting for new code drops | Free | Free-to-play players | Slow, and codes expire |

The practical rule is simple: bank your Grade Tokens, pick one striker as your project, and roll only on that unit. Splitting tokens across five mediocre players produces five mediocre players.

## Upgrades and Potions That Make Rin Pay Off

Upgrades are where a good unit becomes a great one. Two of them — crate value and luck — do most of the heavy lifting, because one multiplies your income and the other multiplies your chances at premium pulls and better grades.

| Upgrade | What it changes | Priority | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crate Value | Cash per crate sold | Highest | Multiplies every crate; compounds with a strong farmer |
| Luck | Odds on hatches and rolls | Highest | More attempts at top-tier pulls and better grades |
| Opening Speed | How fast lockers cycle | Medium | More players per hour means more balls per hour |
| Movement | How fast units move around the base | Medium | Faster collection, less idle time |
| Base Size | How much you can place | Situational | Only worth it once income supports the extra space |

Potions are the accelerant. Pop Luck Potions immediately before a hatch session so the boost covers your best attempts, save Cash Potions for your longest active stretch rather than the ten minutes before you log off, and burn Grade Speed Potions when you are rolling grades in bulk.
