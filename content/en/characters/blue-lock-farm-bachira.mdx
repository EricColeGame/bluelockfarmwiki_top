## Grade Tokens, the Ranking Stand, and Why Grading Matters

Hatching Bachira is only half the job. Grading is where a decent unit becomes a great one, and it happens at the Ranking Stand in the Plaza. You have two ways to roll: spend Cash, or spend Grade Tokens.

Cash rolls look cheap early and get brutal fast. For high-tier units, players report needing millions of Cash per roll, which is a painful drain when you'd rather be buying lockers. Grade Tokens skip that entirely, and since codes hand them out for free, they're the smarter currency to spend on a unit you plan to keep. The UR grade delivers the biggest Cash multiplier of the bunch, which is exactly what you want on a long-term keeper like Bachira.

| Your Situation | Spend Cash? | Spend Grade Tokens? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early game, throwaway units | Yes — rolls are cheap | No, save them |
| Mid-game keeper you'll never sell | Only if you're flush | Yes, preferred |
| Chasing UR on a high-tier unit | Risky — millions per roll | Yes, best possible use |
| Tokens about to expire | — | Redeem and use them |

A simple rule: grade the units you'll still be using next month, and use tokens for the expensive tiers.
