### Why Grade Tokens Are the Sleeper Reward

Grade Tokens deserve their own section because they quietly save you the most money. Grading happens at the Ranking stand in the Plaza, and while you can roll grades with Cash instead, high-tier attempts can run into the millions per roll. The UR grade delivers the biggest Cash multiplier in the game, so every token you bank is a roll you don't have to pay for.

The WEATHER code alone hands you 5 Grade Tokens, and ADMINABUSE adds 20 more. Combined, that's a meaningful chunk of a grading session funded entirely for free.

Trait and Variant rewards work differently — they're tied to player customization systems rather than raw economy. Exact roll odds and outcomes are handled by the game itself, so check the in-game tooltips for current values rather than trusting secondhand numbers.

## The Core Blue Lock Farm Loop: Lockers, Crates, and Cash

Everything in Blue Lock Farm funnels through one cycle: lockers produce players, players produce balls, balls fill crates, and crates convert into Cash. Your job is to make that cycle faster and more valuable at every link in the chain.

| Upgrade | What It Improves | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base size | Floor space for more lockers | High early |
| Crate value | Cash earned per crate sold | High |
| Locker tier | Quality of players you can hatch | High once affordable |
| Luck | Odds on better hatches and rolls | Medium-high |
| Opening speed | How quickly the cycle repeats | Medium |
| Movement speed | How fast players reach and collect balls | Medium |

### Where to Put Your First Million

New players tend to dump Cash into whatever button glows. That's a mistake. A tighter priority order looks like this:

- **Buy the cheapest locker upgrades first.** Early tiers are cheap and give the biggest relative jump.
- **Expand your base before you run out of floor.** Lockers you can't place earn nothing.
- **Push crate value over cosmetic upgrades.** A crate that sells for more pays for everything else.
- **Save luck potions for a dedicated hatch session.** Spreading them across random pulls wastes the multiplier.
- **Bank Grade Tokens instead of burning them on low-tier players.** Grade your best unit, not your newest one.

Players who follow this order generally report reaching a self-sustaining base — one where Cash accumulates faster than they can spend it — noticeably sooner than players who upgrade randomly.

### Mistakes That Stall Progress

- **Redeeming codes before checking spelling.** One wrong character and the code is wasted.
- **Sitting on Potions for "later."** They expire, and later never comes.
- **Neglecting base size.** The fastest way to cap your income is running out of room.
- **Grading everything you hatch.** Grade Tokens are finite; spend them on keepers.
- **Ignoring the Discord.** Community reports consistently show codes appear there first.
- **Chasing the Jackpot Token.** Treat it as a bonus, not a strategy.

One more habit worth building: screenshot your code list before a big session. If a redemption fails, you'll know instantly whether you mistyped it or whether the code simply expired between checks.

## FAQ

**How often does a Blue Lock Farm update drop?**

There's no fixed schedule. Developers typically push new codes alongside milestones, events, and content additions, and the Discord server is the earliest place they surface. Bookmarking a regularly updated code page is the simplest way to stay current without refreshing social feeds all day.

**Are Blue Lock Farm codes free and safe to use?**

Yes. Codes are redeemed inside the game's Shop menu and never require your password, Robux, or any external login. Anyone asking for account details in exchange for codes is running a scam — the real codes are always entered directly in-game.

**What's the fastest way to earn Cash in Blue Lock Farm?**

Fill and sell crates continuously while Cash Potions are active, and prioritize crate value and base size upgrades over everything else. Luck Potions matter most before a dedicated hatching session, since better players generate more balls per minute.

**Do Grade Tokens or Cash matter more?**

Grade Tokens, at least in the early and mid game. Rolling high tiers with Cash can cost millions per attempt, while Grade Tokens let you chase the same UR multiplier for free. Bank every token you can and spend them on players you actually plan to keep.
