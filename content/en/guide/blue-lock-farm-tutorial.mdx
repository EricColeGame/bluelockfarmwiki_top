## What Is Blue Lock Farm? The Core Loop in Plain English

Blue Lock Farm is an anime-inspired Roblox tycoon built around soccer. Instead of managing a restaurant or a shop, you manage a training base: lockers hatch players, those players collect balls, and full crates convert into Cash. That Cash buys better lockers and upgrades, which produce more valuable crates, which produce more Cash.

The genre calls this a "loop," and once it clicks, every decision in the game becomes obvious. If an action does not improve the speed or value of your crates, it is a side quest — fun, but not progress.

| Stage | What You Do | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Buy a locker | Spend Cash to add a locker to your base | Every locker is a permanent income slot |
| 2. Hatch a player | Open the locker to pull a player | Better players mean faster, richer farming |
| 3. Farm balls | Let players collect soccer balls automatically | This runs while you do other things |
| 4. Fill and sell crates | Trade full crates for Cash | This is your only real income source |
| 5. Reinvest | Buy upgrades and better lockers | Compounds your income for the next cycle |

The key insight for new players: your base keeps producing between sessions, so the goal is to leave it in the strongest possible state before you log off.

## Blue Lock Farm Tutorial: Step-by-Step Walkthrough

This Blue Lock Farm tutorial section is the part to follow literally on your first day. Do not skip ahead to cosmetic purchases or rerolling for a perfect unit — the early game rewards volume, not perfection.

| Step | Action | Beginner Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Complete the opening tutorial and claim your first locker | Redeem codes before you start spending |
| 2 | Hatch your first player and watch the crate timer | Learn how long one full crate takes |
| 3 | Sell your first crate and buy a second locker | Two weak lockers usually beat one strong locker early |
| 4 | Buy your first crate value upgrade | This is the single best early purchase |
| 5 | Fill every locker slot before buying cosmetics | Empty slots earn nothing |
| 6 | Add a luck upgrade once income feels stable | Luck pays off over dozens of hatches, not one |
| 7 | Visit the Ranking stand in the Plaza | Grade your best player as soon as you have tokens |

A realistic first session goal is simple: fill every locker you can afford, then push crate value until your next locker purchase takes more than a couple of minutes of farming.

### Early-Game Priorities in Order

- **Codes first.** Free potions and tokens cost you nothing and shave minutes off your opening grind.
- **Crate value second.** A flat boost to every sale beats a lucky hatch you might not get.
- **More lockers third.** Parallel income scales better than perfecting a single unit.
- **Luck fourth.** Luck is a long-term investment, so fund it with stable income.
- **Grading last.** Grade Tokens are limited, so spend them on a player you will keep.

## Locker Tiers, Upgrades, and Where Your Cash Should Go

Lockers come in different price tiers, and each tier hatches players with its own rarity odds. The practical rule is that a locker you cannot afford yet is not automatically better — a cheap locker that fills and sells quickly can outperform an expensive one that sits half-empty.

Upgrades are where most players leak value. There are five upgrade families worth tracking, and they do not deserve equal attention.

| Upgrade Type | What It Improves | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crate Value | Cash earned per crate sold | Highest early and mid game |
| Luck | Odds of hatching stronger players | High, once income is stable |
| Opening Speed | How quickly lockers hatch players | Medium-high |
| Movement | How fast players reach and return balls | Medium |
| Base Size | How many lockers and players fit on your base | Situational — buy when you are slot-capped |

| Phase | What You Should Be Doing | Common Mistake |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early | Fill slots, buy crate value, redeem codes | Saving for one expensive locker too soon |
| Mid | Layer in luck and opening speed, start grading | Ignoring Grade Tokens entirely |
| Late | Chase better lockers and high-tier grades | Selling upgrades short to fund hatches |

Player experience suggests that crate value and luck are the two upgrades you will never regret, because both scale with every locker you own rather than a single unit.

## Grade Tokens and the Ranking Stand Explained

Grade Tokens are the game's ranking currency. You spend them at the **Ranking stand** in the Plaza to roll a grade on a player, which applies a Cash multiplier to everything that player produces. You can also roll with Cash instead, but community reports indicate that high-tier rolls get expensive fast — millions of in-game Cash per attempt once you are chasing top grades.

| Item | What It Does | Smart Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grade Tokens | Roll a player's grade at the Ranking stand | Save for your best long-term players |
| Grade Speed Potions | Speed up the grading process | Use during a dedicated grading session |
| Trait Tokens | Currency for the trait system | Hold until you have a keeper |
| Variant Token | Unlocks a variant outcome | Save for a unit you will actually field |
| Luck Potions | Temporarily boost hatch luck | Stack with a big batch of hatches |
| Cash Potions | Temporarily boost Cash earned | Use while actively farming |
| Jackpot Token | Chance at a bonus reward | Redeem and use immediately |

The UR grade delivers the largest Cash multiplier in the game, which is why Grade Tokens matter so much: enough of them means you can chase top-tier grades without draining your Cash reserves.

## FAQ

**What is the fastest way to make Cash in Blue Lock Farm?**
Fill every locker slot, prioritize crate value upgrades, and sell crates constantly rather than hoarding them. Codes that grant Cash Potions amplify whatever rate you already have, so redeem them when your farm is running, not when it is idle.

**Do Blue Lock Farm codes expire?**
Yes. Most Roblox codes are time-limited or tied to a player milestone, and they can be retired without notice. Redeem anything active as soon as you see it, and treat any code list — including this one — as a snapshot rather than a permanent guarantee.

**What should I spend Grade Tokens on?**
Spend them on players you plan to keep long-term. Rolling a high grade on a unit you will replace tomorrow wastes the multiplier, so grade your best hatches first and let weaker players farm without a grade.

**Is this Blue Lock Farm tutorial useful for returning players?**
Absolutely. If you are coming back after a break, start by checking for new codes, then review your upgrade spread — returning players often find that crate value and luck upgrades have fallen behind their locker count, which caps income even when the base looks full.

The short version: fill your base, feed crate value, redeem every code you can find, and grade only the players worth keeping. Do those four things consistently and your Blue Lock Farm stops being a grind and starts being a machine.
