## How Blue Lock Farm Progression Works

Blue Lock Farm is a soccer-themed Roblox tycoon from developer Finding Illusions. On paper, the loop is simple: buy lockers, hatch players, let those players farm balls, collect full crates, and sell them for Cash. You then pour that Cash into better lockers and upgrades that boost crate value, luck, opening speed, movement speed, and base size.

It compounds beautifully for the first hour or two. Then it doesn't.

| Stage | What you do | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Buy lockers | Spend starting Cash on the cheapest lockers | Your first farmable players |
| 2. Hatch players | Open lockers to roll for new players | Higher ball output per minute |
| 3. Farm balls | Let players auto-farm on the field | Faster crate filling |
| 4. Sell crates | Cash in full crates | Spendable income |
| 5. Upgrade | Crate value, luck, opening speed, movement, base size | Compounding income |
| 6. Grade players | Visit the Ranking stand in the Plaza | Grade-based cash multipliers |
| 7. Reset | Rebirth to convert progress into permanent gains | Long-term scaling |

Every farm eventually hits the same wall. Upgrade prices climb faster than your crate income, and the next meaningful purchase drifts further away with each passing minute. That wall is exactly what a rebirth is designed to break — which is why experienced players plan their reset long before they actually need one.

## What Blue Lock Farm Rebirth Actually Does

A rebirth is a voluntary reset. You trade the farm you have built — lockers, hatched players, unsold crates, and usually your unspent Cash — for a permanent bonus that applies to every run that follows. Instead of grinding the same curve again at the same speed, you restart from a higher baseline and blow past your old ceiling.

Because Roblox tycoons get patched constantly, the exact keep-and-lose rules are set by the developer and can change with any update. Always read the confirmation prompt before you click. Community reports from players who have already reset describe a split along these lines:

| Item | Typical rebirth behavior | What to do about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unspent Cash | Usually wiped | Spend it before you confirm |
| Standard lockers | Usually reset | Don't rebuy expensive lockers right before a reset |
| Hatched players | Usually reset | Pull your best players *after* the reset |
| Unsold crates | Usually cleared | Sell them and reinvest first |
| Grade Tokens | Usually kept | Bank them, then spend on high-tier grades |
| Trait and Variant Tokens | Usually kept | Save them for a player you plan to keep |
| Redeemed code rewards | Usually kept | Redeem every active code beforehand |
| Rebirth multiplier | Permanent | Never lost — this is the whole point |

### When Should You Pull the Trigger?

There is no official countdown, so use judgment. Four quick tests tell you it's time:

- **Income test:** your next meaningful upgrade feels several play sessions away rather than minutes away.
- **Stagnation test:** you finish a full session without buying anything that changes your output.
- **Preparation test:** every active code is redeemed and your tokens are banked.
- **Patch test:** no major update is announced. Updates can change rebirth rewards, so it pays to wait a day if one is imminent.

If three of those four are true, resetting is almost certainly the better play.

The players who get the most out of a reset are the ones who spend ten minutes preparing. Work through this list in order.

| Priority | Task | Why it matters |

Finally, screenshot your base, your stats, and your best pulls. When you are three rebirths deep and wondering whether the loop is still worth it, having a before-and-after record is the only honest way to answer that question.

### Grade Tokens, Traits, and Variant Tokens After a Reset

Grade Tokens are spent at the **Ranking stand** in the Plaza to grade your players. You can also roll grades with Cash, but high-tier units can cost millions per roll — which is exactly why banking tokens matters. The UR grade carries the biggest cash multiplier in the game, and a healthy token stockpile lets you chase it without spending a dime of in-game currency.

Trait Tokens and Variant Tokens work the same way: they are scarce, they usually survive a reset, and they are worth far more once you have a player worth investing in. Hold them until your post-rebirth roster produces a keeper rather than sprinkling them across throwaway pulls. Player experience suggests saving your Jackpot Token until your luck upgrades are rebuilt, so its free-reward roll has the best possible odds behind it.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Blue Lock Farm Rebirth

**What exactly is Blue Lock Farm rebirth?**

It is a voluntary reset of your farm in exchange for a permanent bonus that applies to every future run. You give up lockers, players, unsold crates, and usually your unspent Cash; in return, your next run scales faster than the one before it.

**Do I lose my redeemed code rewards when I rebirth in Blue Lock Farm?**

In most builds, redeemed potions and tokens carry through a reset, which is why the standard advice is to redeem everything *before* you reset. That said, the developer can change these rules in any patch, so read the confirmation prompt carefully before committing.

**How often should I rebirth in Blue Lock Farm?**

Rebirth whenever your income growth stalls — typically when your next meaningful upgrade is sessions away rather than minutes away. There is no reward for resetting early, and no penalty for waiting one more session to bank a few extra tokens.

**What is the fastest way to rebuild after a Blue Lock Farm rebirth?**

Buy the cheapest lockers in bulk, prioritize crate value and luck upgrades, then let your banked Grade Tokens carry you to a high grade on your best player. Add active codes on top and your second run will outpace your first by a wide margin.

The short version: treat Blue Lock Farm rebirth as a planned investment, not a panic button. Prepare, bank your tokens, spend what won't survive, and the reset becomes the single biggest jump your farm ever makes.
