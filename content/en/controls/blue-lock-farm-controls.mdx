### Movement and camera

Roblox's default camera trails behind your character and swings as you move the mouse. For base management, that is usually fine — but two settings change the feel dramatically.

First, **Shift Lock Switch**. Turning this on in Roblox's settings lets you press Shift to lock the camera behind your character. Your avatar then faces wherever the camera points, which makes walking a tight loop around your lockers far more predictable than free-camera steering.

Second, **camera zoom**. Zooming out one or two notches gives you a wider view of your base, which helps you spot which crates are full and which lockers are still producing. Zooming back in helps when you need to click a small prompt.

### Interaction and menus

Interaction in Blue Lock Farm is prompt-driven: walk up to an object and the game surfaces a button. On PC you can click it or, in most cases, tap the interact key instead. Menu navigation runs through on-screen buttons rather than hotkeys — the Shop menu, for example, is where you scroll to the code entry field, and the ranking stand in the Plaza is where Grade Tokens get spent on player grades.

That menu-first design means your right hand spends more time on the mouse than on the keyboard. If you only learn one keybind beyond WASD, make it interact.

## Mobile and Console Controls

Touch and gamepad players get the same game with a different input philosophy. Mobile uses a virtual thumbstick and tappable prompts, while console maps movement to the left stick and the camera to the right.

| Platform | Strengths | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PC (desktop app or browser) | Precise camera, fastest menu clicking, keyboard shortcuts | Requires a mouse for comfortable menu work |
| Mobile (phone) | Play anywhere, tap-to-interact is intuitive | Small buttons, thumbstick drift on worn screens |
| Tablet | Larger touch targets than a phone | Heavier to hold during long sessions |
| Console (gamepad) | Comfortable movement, analog camera | Slower menu navigation, fewer shortcut options |

Mobile players can resize and reposition the on-screen controls from Roblox's mobile settings, which is worth doing if the default layout puts the jump button under your palm. Console players should expect to do more stick-work in menus, since there is no equivalent of a quick mouse click for small UI elements.

Community reports suggest mobile is the most popular way to check in on a farm during the day, while PC remains the platform of choice for long upgrade sessions. Player experience also points to mobile being harder for precise camera work, which matters most when you are hunting for a specific prompt in a crowded Plaza.

## How to Rebind Blue Lock Farm Controls

Blue Lock Farm does not ship a full remapping menu, which is normal for Roblox experiences. What you can change lives in Roblox's own settings, and those changes apply across every game you play — including this one.

| Step | What to do | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open the Roblox menu | Esc on PC, menu icon on mobile or console |
| 2 | Switch Movement Mode | Settings, then choose keyboard and mouse, click-to-move, or touch |
| 3 | Switch Camera Mode | Settings, then pick Classic or Follow |
| 4 | Enable Shift Lock Switch | Settings, then toggle it on to unlock the Shift keybind |
| 5 | Lower Graphics Quality | Settings, then reduce quality if inputs feel delayed |
| 6 | Resize mobile buttons | Roblox mobile settings, then adjust the touch layout |

If you want the official walkthrough for any of these options, Roblox's official help center at [https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us) documents camera modes, movement modes, and accessibility settings in detail.

One note on **Click to Move**: it lets your character walk toward wherever you click, which can be handy for crossing a large base while your attention is on menus. It is a poor fit for tight interaction work, though, because you lose the fine control that WASD gives you.

## Control Tips and Common Fixes

Small adjustments to how you hold and configure your inputs add up over a long farming session. These are the habits that experienced players tend to settle into.

| Tip | Why it helps |
| --- | --- |
| Turn on Shift Lock Switch | Your character faces the camera direction, so walking a base loop becomes muscle memory |
| Zoom out one notch while managing | You can see crate fill levels and locker output at a glance |
| Keep interact on a comfortable key | Fewer misclicks when you are chaining locker purchases |
| Drop graphics quality on older hardware | A steadier framerate makes every input feel more responsive |
| Use Click to Move only for long walks | Combines hands-free travel with precise control when you arrive |
| Play in fullscreen | Browser window edges can steal clicks during menu work |

When something goes wrong, it is usually one of a handful of causes rather than a broken game.

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Character will not move | The chat box has keyboard focus | Click on the 3D view or press Esc to release focus |
| Camera spins or drifts | High sensitivity or controller stick drift | Lower sensitivity in settings, or recalibrate the controller |
| Interact prompt will not trigger | You are out of range or a menu is covering the prompt | Step closer and close open menus |
| Mobile buttons disappeared | UI visibility toggled off | Re-enable the control UI from the menu |
| Inputs feel delayed | Low framerate or background apps | Reduce graphics quality and close other programs |
| Shift does nothing | Shift Lock Switch is disabled | Turn it on in Roblox settings |

One more control consideration ties directly into progression: base **movement** upgrades change how quickly your character gets around. If your layout keeps growing, spending on movement upgrades effectively improves your controls, since a faster avatar covers the same loop in less time.

## FAQ

**What are the default Blue Lock Farm controls on PC?**

Blue Lock Farm controls on PC follow Roblox's standard scheme: WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to aim the camera, Spacebar to jump, and Esc to open the Roblox menu. Interaction prompts can be clicked directly, and many players also use the E key for the same purpose.

**Can you change Blue Lock Farm keybinds?**

There is no in-game remapping menu, so you cannot reassign individual keys. What you can change is everything Roblox controls: movement mode, camera mode, shift lock, graphics quality, and mobile touch layout. Those settings carry across every experience on the platform.

**Do Blue Lock Farm controls work the same on mobile?**

The actions are the same, but the inputs are different — a virtual thumbstick replaces WASD, and swiping replaces mouse aim. Mobile players can resize and reposition their on-screen buttons, which is the closest thing to a custom layout the game offers.

**Why is my interact button not working?**

Nine times out of ten it is range or focus. Step closer to the locker, crate, or ranking stand, close any open menu that might be sitting over the prompt, and make sure the game window has keyboard focus by clicking on the 3D view. If it still fails, rejoin the server — a stale session can leave prompts unresponsive.
